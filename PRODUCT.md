# ScopeQuay

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
The project owner and developers evaluating controlled actions by AI agents.
The user requested a business-style approval dashboard before a research workbench.

## Product Purpose
Review proposed actions, understand the intervention, resolve a request explicitly,
and inspect sandbox effects and their audit trail.

## Operating Context
Public Apache-2.0 GitHub repository and a local Python 3.12 application. The core
research engine already exists. Installed local Ollama models only; no paid APIs.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Five pre-action decisions and recovery in a simulated ledger. New product demo
workflows must be separated from benchmark scores. No real business tools,
authenticated team roles, production authorization, or proven safety improvement.
ScopeQuay replaces the previous public project name at the user's request.

## Evidence on Hand
Versioned synthetic fixtures, recorded Llama runs, optional toolkit slices and
negative calibration findings. No independent annotations or external adoption.

## Product Principles
Show why an action is held. Require explicit approval where needed. Preserve
original evidence. Show simulation status and research limits in plain language.
