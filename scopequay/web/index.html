How ScopeQuay works

  1. Inspect. The agent supplies an action and visible context.
  2. Decide. The gate executes, asks, stages, escalates or refuses.
  3. Review. You explicitly resolve eligible holds. Policy and missing authority cannot be overridden here.
  4. Verify. A separate execution click changes the sandbox; lost acknowledgments and partial effects trigger recovery checks.

The research asks whether calibrated decisions preserve more useful autonomy at a comparable harmful-action rate. An advantage has not been established. This product demo does not generate paper results.
